Our Story

Two homelands,
one table.

Wrap & Roll began with a simple idea: the fragrant, herb-laden street food of Vietnam deserves a home in the calm, forested quiet of Finland. Our founders grew up between Hanoi's morning pho stalls and Helsinki's winter mornings — and built a kitchen that honours both.

Every broth is simmered for at least twelve hours with charred ginger, star anise and roasted bones. Every roll is wrapped by hand. And every dish is plated with the same restraint and clarity you'll find in Nordic design — nothing extra, everything essential.

Two Worlds, One Kitchen

Where Hanoi meets
the Finnish forest.

Vietnam gave us the recipes: bright herbs, patient broths, the ritual of rolling rice paper by hand. Finland gave us the setting: light birch tables, linen napkins, and a stillness that lets the food speak for itself.

We believe good design, like good broth, needs time and restraint. That's why our dining room borrows the calm of a Helsinki winter morning, while every plate carries the colour and spice of a Saigon night market.
